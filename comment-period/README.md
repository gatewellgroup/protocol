# The comment period

Public comment draft, Revision 3. Open through October 31, 2026.

## Questions put for comment

### 1. Evidence for the domestic end product test

Which cost records should evidence the component-cost test of 48 CFR 25.101(a), and for power inverters eligibility under 26 U.S.C. 45X. How supplier cost data a manufacturer cannot compel should be handled. How the computation can be verified while cost data stays confidential.

Clause: 1.5, question 1

### 2. EV supply equipment and the utility-interactive scope

How element (a) of the power-inverter definition, as narrowed by DA 26-870, applies to classes of EV supply equipment, in particular bidirectional equipment that operates in parallel with the electric utility.

Clause: 1.5, question 2

### 3. Calibration of evidence-quality rules

Whether the evidence-age limits, the supplier confirmation counts and the retention periods are workable for mid-size manufacturers.

Clause: 1.5, question 3 , 4.7 , Part V

### 4. Annex D completeness

Whether the deep-trace component matrices omit components that determine a device's character in its category.

Clause: 1.5, question 4 , Annex D

## How to comment

Write to protocol@gatewellgroup.com. A comment that can be acted on gives the clause number, the change proposed and the reason for it. Give your name and affiliation, and say whether the comment may be published with them. By submitting a comment you agree that Gatewell Group may incorporate it into the Protocol and publish it as you have permitted.

## What happens to a comment

Each comment is logged with a number and the date received. Comments are published on this page with the commenter's permission. Every comment receives a written disposition: accepted, accepted in part, or declined with the reason. The disposition table is published with the final text of Version 1.0.

Each question above is open as an issue in this repository (label `comment-period`). A comment on any other clause is opened with the comment form.
